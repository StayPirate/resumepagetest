education

University of Camerino

Camerino — Oct 2011 – Apr 2015

Bachelor's degree, Computer Science.

Thesis: MITM Attack with Patching Binaries on the Fly by Adding Shellcodes — pdf, winner of Innovating Information Security.

My thesis shows how easy it is to achieve remote control of a personal computer — Windows, GNU/Linux or macOS — by intercepting and parsing the traffic of an end system looking for executable codes: if found, a transparent proxy injects the malware before it reaches the end system. A legitimate executable from a trusted server can arrive — during transmission — carrying a backdoor.
